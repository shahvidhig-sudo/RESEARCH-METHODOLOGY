## ⚡ Statistical Tools Lab (Prescribed in Syllabus)
Interactive simulator with pre-loaded nutrition datasets, calculated statistical outputs, live dynamic plots, and copy-ready Nature Portfolio thesis interpretation sentences:

1. **Measures of Central Tendency & Dispersion** (Athlete Protein Intake Distribution: Mean, Median, Mode, Range, Mean Deviation, Standard Deviation $\sigma$)
2. **Student's Independent 't'-Test** (Iron-Fortified vs Control Flour on Hemoglobin $t=3.42, p=0.003, d=1.53$)
3. **Student's Paired 't'-Test** (30-day Low-Glycemic Diet on Fasting Glucose $t=-11.8, p<0.0001$)
4. **Pearson's Chi-Square Test ($\chi^2$)** (Dietary Vitamin A Deficiency vs Bitot's Spots $\chi^2=21.6, p<0.0001, \text{OR}=9.33$)
5. **Simple Linear Correlation ($r$)** (Dietary Sodium Intake vs Systolic Blood Pressure $r=+0.89, p<0.001$)
6. **Simple Linear Regression ($y = mx + c$)** (Predicting Plasma Vitamin C from Citrus Intake: $\hat{y} = 0.53x + 10.2, R^2=0.998$)
7. **One-Way ANOVA** (Serum Calcium across 3 Diets: Dairy, Fortified Soya, Supplement: $F=14.2, p=0.0001$)

## 📚 Syllabus Structure

### Unit 1: Biostatistics
* Types of data (Qualitative vs Quantitative, Discrete vs Continuous).
* Concepts of population and sample; probability vs non-probability sampling.
* Basic probability theory and Gaussian normal distribution.
* Graphical & diagrammatic presentation.
* Data collection methods: Schedules vs Questionnaires, Surveys, Interviews, Case studies, Home visits.
* Reliability & Validity of measuring tools.
* **Access to sequence and nutrient databases on the internet** (NCBI GenBank, UniProt, USDA FoodData Central, Shodhganga).

### Unit 2: Research Methods
* Meaning, objectives, and types of research (Descriptive, Correlational, Explanatory, Exploratory, Basic vs Applied, Deductive vs Inductive, Action Research).
* Problem identification, justification, and hypothesis formulation ($H_0, H_1$).
* Research design features, experimental vs control groups.
* Hypothesis testing and errors (Type I $\alpha$ vs Type II $\beta$).
* Interpretation of tables and figures.
* Thesis writing architecture: Front Matter, Core Body, End Matter & Research Ethics.
